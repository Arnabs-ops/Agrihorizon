# 🚀 New Feature Suggestions for AgriHorizon

Based on the current codebase analysis, here are feature ideas that would enhance the platform:

## 💰 Payment & Financial Features

### 1. **Escrow System**
- Hold payments in escrow until delivery confirmation
- Automatic release after delivery + 24-hour dispute window
- Protects both buyers and sellers

### 2. **Payment Installments**
- Allow buyers to pay in 2-3 installments for large orders
- Automatic reminders and payment scheduling
- Interest-free or low-interest options

### 3. **Seller Payment Analytics**
- Revenue trends, payment success rates
- Peak payment times, average transaction values
- Payment method preferences

### 4. **Buyer Payment History & Receipts**
- Downloadable payment receipts
- Tax invoice generation
- Payment history dashboard

### 5. **Loyalty Points & Rewards**
- Earn points on purchases
- Redeem points for discounts
- Referral bonuses

## 📦 Order & Delivery Features

### 6. **Real-Time Order Tracking**
- GPS-based live tracking (integrate with delivery partner APIs)
- Estimated delivery time updates
- Delivery notifications

### 7. **Bulk Order Discounts**
- Automatic tiered pricing (partially exists)
- Volume-based discounts for wholesale buyers
- Group buying features

### 8. **Order Scheduling**
- Schedule orders for future dates
- Recurring orders (weekly/monthly)
- Harvest-based automatic ordering

### 9. **Multi-Product Orders**
- Cart system improvements
- Bundle deals
- Cross-seller order consolidation

### 10. **Delivery Slot Booking**
- Choose delivery time slots
- Express delivery option
- Same-day delivery for local orders

## 🌾 Agriculture-Specific Features
